Two ways we look after your child

Every child is welcome here. Whether yours is just starting out or already in high school, there is a place for them at Toddler's Town.

Ages 2 to 5

Day care

Structured early childhood development and play-based learning. Children explore letters, numbers, puzzles and stories, make friends and build confidence in a safe, caring space.

  • Play-based learning every day
  • Teachers who treat each child like their own
  • Daily updates for parents
Grade 1 to 9

After care

A calm place to go after school. Our teachers help learners finish their homework, work on school projects and prepare for exams, so evenings at home are stress-free.

  • Homework assistance
  • Support with school projects
  • Exam preparation
